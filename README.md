Bhu Khata (भू खाता)

Smart India Hackathon 2026 - Problem Statement 26018

Problem Statement: 
Intelligent Land Record Digitization and Validation System Ministry of Rural Development, Department of Land Resources

The Problem:
Millions of legacy land records across India - Jamabandi, Record of Rights (RoR), 7/12 extracts, Patta/Chitta - still exist only as scanned or physical documents in regional languages and formats. Digitizing them manually is slow, expensive, and error-prone, slowing down DILRMP's national land record modernization effort.

What This Does:
Bhu Khata reads a scanned land record (image or PDF) and automatically extracts structured information - landowner details, Khasra/Khata numbers, plot area, village/tehsil/district, ownership shares, and more - directly from the document. Extracted data is validated against real cadastral business rules (area totals, ownership share sums, duplicate plot detection), and anything the system isn't confident about is flagged for quick human review rather than accepted blindly.
Every human correction is remembered and used to improve future extractions on similar documents - the system gets more accurate the more it's used, without retraining.

Key Capabilities: 
-> Multilingual document recognition (Hindi, English,and more)

-> Structured extraction from scanned images and PDFs

-> Automated validation: area aggregation, ownership share checks, duplicate parcel detection, multi-parcel holding flags

-> Confidence scoring with automatic flagging of uncertain fields

-> Human-in-the-loop verification workflow

-> Self-improving extraction via a correction-feedback loop

-> Interactive dashboard: documents processed, extraction accuracy, pending verification, validation status

-> Cadastral search by Khasra/Survey number, owner name, or location

Status:
Working prototype, built for SIH 2026 internal and national rounds.
